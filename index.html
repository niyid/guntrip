The Inheritance

📖 7 chapters · 🌍 7 wars · 🔫 1 rifle · ⏳ 60 years

A rifle changes hands nine times across sixty years and seven wars before it ends a family on a yacht in the Gulf of Guinea. The men who kill them have no idea the gun's first shot, sixty years earlier, was fired for the grandfather of the man they just killed. No character in this story is ever named — in war, the story argues, a name is the first thing you lose.

Korea '53→ Vietnam→ Cambodia→ Afghanistan→ Sudan→ Libya→ Liberia→ Nigeria

About this project — THE INHERITANCE exists as both a novel and a screenplay. Chapters post here in a mix of story order and standalone highlights — check each entry's week number for the intended reading sequence. New chapter every week.
